Pony Parties at Your Location

Our beautiful ponies are the perfect addition to your party!
We bring two, three, four or more of our wonderful ponies to your location, for any occasion.
All they need is a small yard, cul-de-sac, sidewalk, quiet street, parking lot or even a park.
We can bring our ponies to your house, local park or almost any location of your choice!
We hand-walk them, and parents are always welcome to walk along with their child.
We dress up our ponies to match any theme you can dream of, including unicorns, and My Little Pony!
We instantly provide all cleanup after the ponies, leaving you with only wonderful memories.
All of our ponies are super sweet, gentle, safe, sparkling clean and healthy. They love their jobs and children young or old! Recommended for ages 1-100! (Yes, we do “grown-up” parties and events too!)
We lovingly donate a portion of our pony party proceeds towards rescuing horses and ponies in need, and towards our therapy services helping children and seniors with chronic and terminal illnesses.
