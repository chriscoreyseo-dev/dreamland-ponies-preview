Unicorn Rides in the Park

We are excited to announce we are now offering private Unicorn rides in Maple Valley, WA! ​​
Our beautiful unicorn rides are the perfect, safe outdoor activity for your unicorn obsessed child or children! Offered weekdays only, Monday through Friday. Great entertainment and magical memories for children ages 1 through 8 years old.

Come join us for a magical unicorn experience in Maple Valley, WA at our beautiful local natural park! (The address of the park location will be provided to you upon completion of booking your reservation!)

​We are open year-round, and rain or shine, our Unicorns don't mind!

We hand-walk our Unicorns, and parents are always welcome to walk alongside with their child.

All of our Unicorns are super sweet, gentle, safe, sparkling clean and healthy. They love giving rides, love getting attention, and love children! 

​We lovingly donate a portion of our Unicorn ride proceeds towards our horse and pony rescue, and our therapy services helping children and seniors with chronic and terminal illnesses. 
Unicorn Ride Packages:

  • Includes one Unicorn for one child for a 30 minute private pony ride in the park located in Maple Valley, WA. 
  • Our Unicorn ride comes with a professionally trained handler, and one adorable Unicorn dressed in a beautiful, custom sparkly saddle, saddle pad color of your choice, and glitter in mane and tail, for riding, petting, leading, hugging and taking pictures with, for one child, for 30 minutes. Offered weekdays only, Monday through Friday. 
  • Rider weight limit for our small unicorns is 60lbs maximum. (Please see our large Unicorn and full-size Unicorn options for riders weighing over 60lbs.) 
  • Additional Add-On Options available: Pegasus

Offered weekdays only, Monday through Friday. 
  • Rider weight limit for our small unicorns is 60lbs maximum. (Please see our large Unicorn and full-size Unicorn options for riders weighing over 60lbs.)
