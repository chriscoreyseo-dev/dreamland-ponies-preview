About Us

Dreamland Ponies was designed to be more than just a regular business... It was created to become the crème de la crème of entertainment. ​Located in Maple Valley, WA, Dreamland Ponies is a full-service, family operated pony party, petting zoo, and entertainment business, consistently voted Best of Seattle’s Children's Entertainment company for 6 years in a row. 

Founded by Sarah Kessler, a proud mother, respected business owner, event organizer, philanthropist, animal rescuer, equine therapy provider, riding instructor, dreamer and doer, with over 25 years of experience training and working with horses and animals.

About the Owner

Dreamland Ponies' owner Sarah is a veterinary technician, horse and animal rescuer, animal lover, mother, entrepreneur, philanthropist, and business owner. She grew up rescuing animals, riding horses, competing in Three Day Eventing and Show Jumping, and providing lessons and equine therapy at the early age of 14. 
 
Today, Sarah is on a mission to help, inspire and touch lives by bringing joy to as many people as possible and creating a positive impact on the world. She works in unison with multiple nonprofits, charities, hospitals, nursing homes, and schools – it’s her true passion. 

​Known as a dynamic person with a warm and charismatic personality, Sarah lives life to the beat of a fast pulse. Whether it’s rescuing animals, touching a special needs child's life, bringing a smile to those in need, or launching new business ventures, she can’t help but make a name for herself catering to requests from Seattle’s big names, giving back or challenging herself. It’s in her DNA.

Our Services:
We designed a vast portfolio of premium services that cater to your every need – here’s a quick recap:  

Pony Parties: The perfect choice for children and adults! We bring our beautiful ponies to add elegance and a touch of creativity to any party. And the best part? We dress them to match any theme ranging from Unicorns, to Pegasus, to Princesses and Fairies, to real-life My Little Ponies, you name it. 

​Pony Entertainment: Community Events, Festivals, Grand Openings, Church Socials, Company Picnics, Schools, Fundraisers, Pony Grams, Ponies for Television, Media, Film or any Special Occasion – our ponies will brighten up any event and make it truly memorable. 
​
Pony Pictures: There are moments worth capturing. Our pony photography services will create memories your children will cherish for a lifetime.

Unicorns: From fairy tale unicorns, white unicorns and rainbow unicorns, to mini and full-size unicorns, these fascinating unicorns will take your children on a journey to a new world.

Weddings: The perfect choice for a truly majestic wedding that will keep your guests talking about it for years! Let our magical white horses and ponies create a memorable experience that will make this day even more special. 

Equine Education Programs: We teach children in a fun and hands-on environment about safety around horses, caring for horses, riding horses, proper tack, different horse and pony breeds and so much more!

Pony Therapy: Most of our ponies and miniature horses are licensed therapy animals that love touching lives by visiting children and seniors in hospitals, assisted living centers, rehabilitation centers even private homes. Let these sweet animals join efforts to contribute to a more effective therapeutic experience. 

Cinderella Carriage: A gorgeous, custom pony sized Cinderella Carriage designed for the most demanding princesses to make their day truly special.  

​Dreamland Pony Express: An amazing trackless train that adds a touch of fun and originality to any party or event.
