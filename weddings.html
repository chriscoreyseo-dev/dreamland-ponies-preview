Dreamland Ponies Weddings

Enhance your wedding and make the entrance that dreams are made of... Have our gorgeous, majestic white horses, ponies or unicorns carry you, your flower girl or ring bearer down the aisle, or bring them in with our stunning white Cinderella carriage, hand-led by a beautiful attendant dressed to suit.  

Your wedding marks the beginning of a new phase in your life – a special day that unites two souls, forever. You want it to be magical. This is when Dreamland Ponies comes in. 

We specialize in delivering the crème de la crème of dreams and entertainment, creating memorable experiences, and crafting a special atmosphere of magic and happiness swirling in the air. 

Make the entrance you dreamed of with our majestic white horses, ponies, and unicorns down the aisle. From our white ponies and unicorns to our pristine white horses and Cinderella carriage, we are equipped to create a truly memorable experience that you will be proud to share. 

​Dreamland Ponies creates the right balance between luxury and magic in a wedding package that is the cherry on top of a magnificent day to celebrate this happy ever after moment.
