Discover the best Fundraising Ideas for school fundraisers, church fundraising, youth sports fundraising, and non-profit or charity event fundraisers. We help schools, churches, and non-profit groups raise more funds.

How to advertise your fundraising event
Getting the word out about your event is critical for success. Here's a list of a few places you could advertise your fundraising event:  
  • Word of mouth – tell everyone you know about what you are doing.
  • Facebook, Twitter, and other social media.
  • Blogs.
  • Local radio stations – most local stations have a free community notice events announcement slot.
  • Local newspapers and newsletters.
  • Church, school or local supermarket notice boards.
  • Create posters to put up around your area.
 
Hire our ponies for your event so we can help support you
We have an extensive contact network and highly active Facebook page and would be happy to help spread the word about your fundraising efforts.

To hire our ponies for your event, simply Contact Us with all the details, including the type of event, when (date and time), where and how much you hope to raise... After we receive the details about your event, we will send you a free personalized quote to have our ponies help support you!

Don't forget to tell everyone how your event went
Once it's all over, get back in touch with your supporters to let them know how your event went. Be sure to take a few photographs during the event to help show people what a success it was. Feel free to send us an email once it all wraps up with full details of your event and how much you raised, so we can publicly thank you for your efforts, too.
