Welcome to Dreamland Unicorn Parties...

Our real-life, majestic unicorns are available for hire, ready to make dreams come true while creating an atmosphere filled with magic, fun, and happiness.  

Whether you are looking to brighten up a special day, add a touch of creativity and elegance to any event, or simply make any occasion instantly magical, our real-life unicorns are ready to help you by bringing in their aura of beauty, magic, and fantasy. Children love them and adults are always mesmerized by their beauty and elegance as they play graciously. These pristine unicorns add that spark of magic to any event ranging from photo shoots and unicorn parties, to unicorn rides and so much more!

At Dreamland Unicorns, we have fairy tale unicorns, white unicorns, rainbow unicorns, mini unicorns, pony unicorns, full-size unicorns and everything in between. You name it and we will make it happen. In our hands, your dreams can easily become a reality....
