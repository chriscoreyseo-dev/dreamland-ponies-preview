Corporate Events 
Having a pony or unicorn at your next company event is a perfect way to engage your guests. Our pony entertainment is great for all ages and can be used at staff parties, trade shows, company picnics, holiday parties, conferences, meetings, dinners, and fundraisers. It can also be used to increase brand awareness with our various add-on services. And that's not all - we also offer a fairytale petting zoo that will captivate and delight your guests. Interact with adorable animals straight out of a storybook, creating an even more magical experience for everyone to enjoy.

Seamless Event Management:
No matter the size of your event, whether it's an intimate gathering or a grand affair for thousands, Dreamland Ponies has the experience and expertise to handle all of your needs. Let us take care of every detail, ensuring your event runs smoothly and leaves a lasting impression.

Unique Grand Openings: 
Make your business or special promotion stand out with our captivating pony attractions. While kids delight in riding, petting, and picture opportunities with our adorable ponies, parents can fully immerse themselves in shopping and engaging with your brand.

Company Picnics & Employee Appreciation:
Elevate your company picnics or employee appreciation events to new heights with our comprehensive offerings. From exciting pony rides to engaging activities and the enchanting fairytale petting zoo, we'll help you create the best event your team will cherish.

Unforgettable Corporate Barbecue
Embrace the outdoors and foster team spirit with a delightful corporate barbecue. Our pony companions and the fairytale petting zoo can join in the fun, making the occasion all the more unique and enjoyable for your colleagues.

Corporate Holiday Parties:
Celebrate the holiday season in style, show your hardworking employees how much you value their dedication with a corporate holiday party like no other. Our festive ponies and unicorn, along with the fairytale petting zoo, add a touch of magic to the celebrations, spreading joy and creating lasting memories.

Pony Marketing and Advertisement:
Looking to boost brand awareness in an innovative way? Picture this: a cute pony (or unicorn) standing proudly with your business banner, attracting attention like never before. Your customers won't be able to resist snapping "FREE PONY PICTURES" with your business name. This ingenious advertising approach, combined with the fairytale petting zoo, will have them reminiscing about the experience for years to come.
