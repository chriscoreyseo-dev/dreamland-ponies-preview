Pony Rides in the Park

We are excited to announce we are now offering private hand-led pony rides in Maple Valley, WA! ​​
Our beautiful pony and unicorn rides are the perfect, safe outdoor activity for your pony, horse, and unicorn obsessed child or children! Offered weekdays only, Monday through Friday. Great entertainment and magical memories for children ages 1 through 8 years old.

Come join us for a magical unicorn or pony ride experience in Maple Valley, WA at our beautiful local natural park! (The address of the park location will be provided to you upon completion of booking your reservation!)

​We are open year-round, and rain or shine, our ponies don't mind!

We hand-walk our ponies, and parents are always welcome to walk alongside with their child.

All of our ponies are super sweet, gentle, safe, sparkling clean and healthy. They love giving pony rides, love getting attention, and love children! 

​We lovingly donate a portion of our pony ride proceeds towards our horse and pony rescue, and our therapy services helping children and seniors with chronic and terminal illnesses.
