Dreamland Ponies Pony Grams!

​How would you like to brighten someone’s day by sending an adorable pony or unicorn to visit them? Sending a Pony Gram is a new and great way to show someone that you are thinking of them, and the best way to send your funny and important messages to your loved ones. If you’ve got something important or funny to say, let one of our Pony Grams say it for you... Surprise your sweetheart, friend or child with a PONY GRAM!

They are perfect for any occasion from proposals, birthdays, and anniversaries, to asking someone on a date, and even telling someone a new baby is on the way! Our Pony Grams are ideal for any occasion!

Boxes of chocolates are SO overdone! Flowers? Forget it! And Teddy Bears are nice, but a cuddly pony that’s also cute and interactive is sure to be both refreshing and heartwarming. We think ponies are the best – and what better way to spread the word than to send an adorable, sweet pony to your loved one where they can enjoy the best gift EVER?
 
How It Works
Pony-Grams are visits from one of our ponies or miniature horses to someone's office, classroom, dorm or home. This is an excellent way to help prep a student for a rough exam week, remember a birthday or anniversary, or to let someone know that you care. We do all of the work to create an experience that they will never forget.

On your scheduled day, a trained Dreamland Ponies expert and our adorable ponies will deliver your personal message to your special someone in the most memorable way ever. Our pony will deliver your message along with flowers, balloons and other items of your choice to your special someone’s home/work/school. If you also want to send a private message to your loved ones, we can provide cards perfect for every occasion. Moreover, we also have some great balloon packages available. At Dreamland Ponies, we want to fulfill each and every request of our customers and provide them with the ease of getting a complete package in one place. The recipient gets to snuggle, pet, post selfies (#Ponygram), and then snuggle some more. It will be a love fest their friends will envy!

Pony Gram prices start at $399. 
(Additional Add-on options are available to turn your pony into a magical unicorn, pegasus or alicorn)  

All Pony Gram proceeds go to our Rescue Horse and Pony Sanctuary and Pony Therapy Program for special needs children and seniors... You’ll be thoughtful AND philanthropic!

Additional Pony Gram Options
Pony Grams are a great fundraiser for businesses and schools! Chances are, come Valentine’s Day students at your school are doing some sort of fundraiser that involves delivery of candy or flowers to that special someone (or secret crush they’ve been eyeing from across the room during class).

Pony Gram Terms & Conditions…
The Fine Print                                                                                                   
To qualify, the recipient’s home, school or workplace must be within 15 miles of our Maple Valley Farm location, or a travel fee will apply. We’ll also need confirmation that it’s A-OK for a pony to come inside your Sweetheart or special person’s workplace or school. Pony Grams are are scheduled for 20-minute sessions. You must make sure the recipient will be there. Please try to have a spare room, office, conference area, etc. where pony pictures and petting time can take place.

Dreamland Ponies is not responsible for damages incurred to property or participants during a Pony Gram visit. 

Dreamland Ponies reserves the right to use any and all photos and videos, taken during the regular course of business, for social media, website use and promotional materials. If a participant submits a written request to be left out of these materials, every reasonable action will be taken to ensure that said person's face and any distinguishing features are not present in any of our materials.

Dreamland Ponies will arrive at Pony Gram appointments approximately 15-minutes before your designated/scheduled time. Our staff will keep in close communication with you by calling or texting with our approximate time of arrival, to help make it the best surprise of a lifetime!
