Cinderella Carriage

​Let your happily ever after begin with us... 
​A romantic and whimsical Cinderella carriage is the most glamorous accessory you can bring to an event... And it is with our great pleasure to offer you our custom, gorgeous, one-of-a-kind miniature Cinderella Carriage straight out of a storybook. Our magical carriage is guaranteed to create and ensure you have the fairy-tale experience of your dreams.
​Our beautiful pony-drawn carriage is available for any special occasion or celebration, for princesses of any age. We specialize in creating once in a lifetime magical experiences for you, full of unforgettable memories and dreams come true... For birthdays, Quinceaneras, Sweet 16, proposals, weddings, anniversaries, baby showers, parties, fairs, festivals, parades, holidays, photo shoots, advertising, and marketing or anything you can dream of... 

Our fairy tale carriage is painted pristine white and comes lavishly and beautifully decorated with gorgeous white silk flowers and bows, or we can custom tailor and decorate it to match your special event's design, theme, and color. If you can dream it... We can create it!

​We can place a professionally made sign on the back of our carriage appropriate to your event displaying; "Just Married", "Happy Birthday", "Happy Anniversary", "It's A Girl", your company logo or message, et cetera. We can also have your sign personalized for you to keep for a nominal fee.

All of Dreamland Ponies enchanted ponies are lovely, beautiful, gentle, love people and their jobs. They are experienced working in crowds and traffic situations, and are completely safe and dependable. Our ponies, carriage, and harnesses are sparkling clean. The ponies we use are radiant white with fairy tale manes and tails. We can leave them natural colored, or paint their hooves gloss black, silver, or gold; and can temporarily color their manes and tails any color with safe, non-toxic, veterinarian approved, water-based paint. We can weave flowers and/or ribbons into the mane or adorn their bridle with a feather plume for a look of royalty.

Our ponies and carriage are hand-led and driven by a highly qualified, professional coachman/attendant with many years of experience working with horses and ponies in the public. Our friendly attendant will come dressed in professional, formal or semi-formal attire or tailored to your event design, as you wish. 

​When it comes to your special event, we know there are no second chances, no do-overs and no re-shoots. We know how stressful event planning and preparations are. But you can rely on Dreamland Ponies to deliver the finest of service, ponies, and staff. We are an 18-year-old award-winning company with a flawless history. We are the best in our industry, dependable, knowledgeable and friendly. Our number one priority is the safety and happiness of our clients and our beloved animals. We will provide you with a personal and professional experience that will exceed your expectations, and make all of your princess' dreams come true.
