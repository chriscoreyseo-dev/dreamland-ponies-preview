Welcome to Dreamland Ponies

“Making dreams come true is what we do”

Dreamland Ponies is an award winning company and the greater Seattle area's choice for premier, upscale pony rides, parties, petting zoos, and entertainment. Dreamland Ponies provides stunning, immaculate, show quality, sweet, and extremely gentle ponies... Perfect for parties or celebrations at your home, school, church or public event.

Dreamland Ponies lovingly donates a portion of all proceeds towards our horse and pony rescue, and our therapy services helping children and seniors with chronic and terminal illnesses.​ We provide our elite pony and petting zoo services to clients located in Western Washington State, and are fully licensed and insured.

Pony Parties
Dreamland Ponies are the perfect addition to any party for both children and adults! ​We bring our beautiful, sparkling clean, show quality ponies to you. We dress our ponies to match any theme you can dream of including My Little Pony, Unicorns, Pegasus, Princess, Fairies and more!
Pony Rides
There is nothing more magical or memorable for a child than a real life Dreamland Pony Ride Experience... Petting, grooming, leading, and riding on the back of a pony is a dream come true for children of all ages....   
Unicorns
Real-life, majestic unicorns for hire!
Fairy tale unicorns, white unicorns, rainbow unicorns, mini unicorns, full-size unicorns and everything in between.... Available for all of your wishes, hopes and dreams. Unicorn rides, unicorn parties, unicorn photo shoots and more....
Cinderella Carriage
Dreamland Ponies is proud to announce our beautiful, one of a kind, custom miniature "pony sized" Cinderella Carriage for the little princess in your life. Young or old, Birthdays, Sweet 16,  Quinceaneras or weddings....
Petting Zoo
We have fairytale miniature goats, wooly goats, soft bunnies, fuzzy guinea pigs, fluffy chickens, a tiny mini pig, and a tiny mini horse in our petting zoo! Our petting zoo animals are pampered and handled daily since birth, and are very docile and gentle. They all love to be petted, cuddled and loved. ​
Pony Pictures
Children have always loved ponies and having their picture taken on a pony... A tradition going back over 100 years! Dreamland Ponies has helped continue this wonderful tradition with their pony photography services, which create memories you will cherish for a lifetime.
Pony Entertainment
Our beautiful ponies are available for hire, year-round at the location of your choice. Our ponies are wonderful for Community Events, Festivals, TV, Film, Media, Grand Openings, Church Socials, Company Picnics, Schools, Fundraisers or any Special Occasion. 
Pony Therapy
Most of our ponies and miniature horses are licensed therapy animals that love touching lives by visiting children and seniors in hospitals, assisted living centers, rehabilitation centers, hospice and even in private homes....
Weddings
Enhance your wedding and make the entrance that dreams are made of... Have our gorgeous, majestic white horses, ponies or unicorns carry you, your flower girl or ring bearer down the aisle, or bring them in  with our stunning white Cinderella carriage, led by a beautiful attendant dressed to suit. 
Equine Education
Teaching children a solid foundation of equine knowledge is something we are very passionate about it... Starting from the ground up, we teach children hands- on about safety around horses, caring for horses, riding horses, proper tack, different horse and pony breeds, parts of the horse and more!
