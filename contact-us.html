Hello! Thank you for your interest in Dreamland Ponies. Yes, we are open and would love to hear from you! Please fill out the form below to say hi, ask questions, or book your event. 

​Dreamland Ponies is based in Maple Valley, Washington, USA. We make in person appearances in King County, Kitsap County, Pierce County, Snohomish County, Thurston County, and serve The Greater Seattle Area of Washington State. If you are located outside of our travel radius you are welcome to make a reservation with our ponies and unicorns at our local public park location in Maple Valley, WA.

​If you have questions about pricing, location suitability, and party planning considerations please visit our Service and FAQ pages.​

For all other questions, or to make a reservation, please complete all fields on this form. If you haven’t already, please view the below pages before filling out an inquiry to become familiar with what we offer. You will need to scroll down to do so and click the submit button at the bottom. You are also welcome to send us an email at Hello@Dreamlandponies.com, or call 888-960-PONY (Toll Free).   

We are a family owned and operated business; we may be working at events, with another pony party client, or outside working on our farm caring for our ponies. We try to respond as quickly as possible, but sometimes we cannot reply right away... Please give us 24-48 hours to respond. 😊
 ​
Thank you for choosing Dreamland Ponies, we look forward to making all of your magical pony and unicorn dreams come true!
