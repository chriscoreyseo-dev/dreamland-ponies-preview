Petting Zoo at Your Location! 

​Welcome to Dreamland Ponies Petting Zoo! Our Petting Zoo is no ordinary zoo... We have beautiful, majestic fairytale animals including miniature goats, wooly goats, soft bunnies, fuzzy guinea pigs, fluffy chickens, a tiny mini horse, a tiny mini pig, a mini alpaca, a mini donkey, and small tortoise, and a giant tortoise! This is a mobile-only petting zoo, available at your venue or location!

Our petting zoo animals are beautiful, pampered and have been handled daily since birth. They are very docile, gentle, love people and getting attention. They can be petted, hugged, loved and pose with you for photos... They are gorgeous, show quality animals, photo and film perfect, and have been featured in magazines, commercials and advertisements.

Our beautiful animals are the perfect addition to your party. We bring two, three, four or more of our wonderful animals to your location, for any occasion. All they need is a small yard, garage, cul-de-sac, sidewalk, quiet street, parking lot or even a park.

We provide instant cleanup after our petting zoo animals, leaving you with only wonderful memories.
All of our animals are super sweet, gentle, safe, sparkling clean and healthy. They love their jobs and children young or old! Recommended for ages 1-100! (Yes, we do “grown-up” parties and events too!)

All Petting zoo options include elegant and safe petting zoo fencing, expert petting zoo attendants, premium artificial grass rugs, flower decorations and baskets. Elegant white canopies are available for rent as an add-on, in case of inclement weather or needed shade. We arrive a minimum of one hour early to set up your petting zoo.
