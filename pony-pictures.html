Pony Pictures

Daycare, Preschool and School Western Pony Pictures:

Welcome to Dreamland Pony Pictures! Where we offer fantastic, professional Western pony pictures at your location!!

Join in the fun and excitement of having Dreamland Ponies’ Pony Pictures taken at your daycare, preschool, kindergarten or childcare center location! Our ponies are a spectacular field trip that comes to you, and our pony pictures are a great way for your school to raise money at the same time!
 
Each pony photo session includes cuddles, kisses and an exclusive interaction with our very own special ponies. Our ponies are child-friendly, extremely gentle, and great with Special Needs children. We always bring two ponies to your center… One pony for the children to sit on and have their photo taken on, and a tiny miniature horse for children to interact with and pet while they are waiting in line! (All children will get a turn to pet our tiny mini horse regardless if pictures are ordered for them or not, we never want to make a child feel left out!)

(Please note... Pony rides are NOT included on picture day. If you would like children at your center to ride ponies while we are taking photos, it is $599 per hour for one pony, or $749 per hour for two ponies. Your center must provide staff to assist with loading and unloading the children on and off the riding pony and picture pony.)
 
Our photographers are fun, experienced professionals that love kids, ponies and working with them together! Your children are always safe with our trusted professionals... Our photographers pass an extensive background check and are specially trained to work with ponies, infants, toddlers, and young children in preschool and daycare center settings. They make sure that every child feels calm, relaxed, and safe around our ponies before, during and after having their photo taken. Our photographers know how to create stunning pony pictures, and are creative at bringing out the best smiles, and biggest laughs. We provide puppets to help capture smiles, and each child gets a special pony sticker after their picture is taken.
 
Our pony photographers arrive equipped with the latest in digital photography tools to shoot the highest quality photos possible. We bring the studio to you with  state-of-the-art digital photography equipment. We provide high-quality western outfits, transforming “every day" kids into instant, darling little cowboys and cowgirls! Our costumes include real leather suede chaps and vests, a denim shirt, cowboy hat, and bandana.  
 
We provide prepay order forms (just like with regular school pictures) for parents to order our Pony Pictures. There is no cost to your school, and there is no travel, sitting or minimum order fees. Parents love our pictures but are under no obligation to purchase any photo package. Our minimum package prices start at $79. 
 
At Dreamland Ponies, giving back to our schools and helping children is very important to us, and is part of our mission. We sincerely appreciate the hard work you do, and the wonderful services you provide in caring for children. As a way of us saying thank you for all that you do, we offer programs to help your school raise money by donating a portion of our proceeds from your center’s pony pictures, directly back to your school. This money can go into early childhood educational programs, scholarships for parents or children, or anything your school needs. Please contact us for more information how our pony pictures program can help raise money for your school!
 
How Does it Work?
 
Step 1: Contact us!
We want your pony picture day to be a simple and joyful experience for you, your staff and your families, so we first need to find out about your center’s size, location and any needs your center may have. We can then evaluate how we can best provide you with our pony picture services, and what day works best for your pony picture day!

Please fill out our contact us form or simply call us to schedule a fantastic pony picture session at your center or school. (Our pony pictures are great for preschools, daycares, childcare centers, kindergarten, elementary schools, libraries, church programs, and toddler programs! Ages 2-7 work best for our pony pictures. Our minimum package prices start at $79. We require a MINIMUM of 25 pre-paid orders to bring our picture pony services to your center.)
 
Step 2: Promote Your Picture Day!
1 to 3 weeks before pony picture day arrives, we will get you and your families ready for our pony picture day by providing promotional materials and schedules with all the details parents need to know. This includes posters, flyer notices, email communications for you to send to your families, and order forms for families to return to your center with their payment on pony picture day.
 
Step 3: Send in Your Class Roster
Please download and print the PDF template here that you can use to start getting your class rosters ready. We need each child’s first and last name, parent email addresses, and their teacher's name. Rosters must be submitted a minimum of two weeks before your pony picture day.

Step 4: The Week Before...
The week before, please use our flyers and email communications provided for you, to remind all parents that order forms and payment is due in one week, on your scheduled pony picture day.

Step 5: The Day Before...
The day before your scheduled pony picture day, please put your fun pony reminder stickers on children's clothing.  Please remember to collect order forms and payments from parents on or before the day of your scheduled pony picture day. (Our pony pictures are only available with prepaid orders.)  

Step 6: Pony Picture Day is Finally Here!!
The day you have been waiting for has finally arrived…The ponies are here, the ponies are here!!

On your center’s scheduled pony picture day, we bring our photography studio to you... Our photographer arrives early to set up everything necessary to create the best pony pictures ever possible. We have the ability to take pony pictures inside or out (which will be arranged in advance of your pony picture day). For outdoor photos, our experienced, professional photographer will choose an appropriate area at your location with the best background for the photos. For indoor pony pictures, we provide a fantastic backdrop, and large faux grass mat for the picture pony to stand on. (No worries… our ponies are used to being indoors and are potty trained! However, we do realize that accidents can sometimes happen, and we come fully prepared with clean-up equipment to immediately clean up any mess if needed. We leave your establishment in as pristine condition as it was when we arrive!)

After setting up our portable studio at the chosen location, we have your school staff assist each child to get dressed in a complete cowboy/cowgirl costume. These include a long sleeved western denim shirt, a cowboy hat, real suede leather vest and chaps, and a red bandana. Your staff then helps to place the children on and off the photo pony. (We require your staff to assist with dressing the children up, and placing the children and off the pony to lessen "Stranger danger" and also possible  fear of sitting on a live pony for the first time!) We take 2-4 poses of each child sitting on our pony to make sure we capture the very best one. Our photographer will work together with you to ensure a fun, smooth and joyful pony picture day experience. 

*Please make sure to remember to have enough staff to assist the children the entire time we are onsite. Please also make sure to provide our photographer with all order forms and payments as soon as they arrive at your location, prior to them setting up for your photo shoot!

Step 7: Pony Picture Packages Arrive
Pony picture packages will arrive at your center within three to four weeks after pony picture day, and your families will be smiling from ear to ear! 100% satisfaction is guaranteed. We offer a money back guarantee if parents are not completely satisfied with our pony pictures, or a retake day at larger schools.
 
To help us to continue to improve your pony picture experience, you will receive a quick survey to give us any feedback. We value your opinion as we strive to grow and improve our pony photography program.
